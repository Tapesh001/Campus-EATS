Campus EATS

[kam·pəs ēts] noun, plural · almost a lifestyle

From the student, “what should we even eat?” 1. Every campus kitchen on one screen, from midnight Maggi to a proper paneer roll. 2. The art of ordering before you reach the counter, then strolling past the queue like you own the place. 3. A group chat that starts with “just one snack” and ends with three shops and a feast for six.

Synonyms: canteen run, hostel-gate feast, exam-week fuel
